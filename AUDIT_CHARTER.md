## 0. Preamble & Operational Scope

### 0.1 Five International Academic Standards Unified Under One Rubric

All mathematical and forensic audits in [`ultari-proof-anatomy`](./README.md) are evaluated against a **5-Gate Audit Rubric** (`Open Proof Forensic Framework`, `OPFF`) synthesizing published guidelines from five international communities across publication ethics, pure mathematics, formal verification artifacts, authorship transparency, and machine learning reproducibility:

1. **COPE** (*Committee on Publication Ethics* : *COPE Core Practices*, *Retraction Guidelines*, *Position Statement on Authorship and AI Tools*)
2. **IMU / AMS / EMS** (*International Mathematical Union*, *American Mathematical Society*, *European Mathematical Society* : *AMS Ethical Guidelines* & *IMU Best Practices for Journals*)
3. **ACM / SIGPLAN** (*Association for Computing Machinery* : *ACM Artifact Review and Badging Policy v1.1*)
4. **ICMJE / NISO** (*International Committee of Medical Journal Editors* & *ANSI/NISO Z39.104 CRediT Taxonomy* : *ICMJE Recommendations on AI-Assisted Technology*)
5. **NeurIPS / ICML / ICLR** (*Three Major AI Conferences* : *Code of Ethics* & *Paper Reproducibility Checklist*)

### 0.2 Legislative Rationale: Four Structural Blind Spots and the Necessity of Industrialized Proof Forensics

Although frontier AI laboratories have begun releasing formal verification (`Lean 4`) corpora and mathematical preprints spanning tens of thousands to millions of lines, traditional academic oversight lacked an automated cross-check mechanism due to **four intersecting structural blind spots**:

1. **The Assumption That `"0 sorry = Specification Conformance"`**:
   A formal proof kernel (`Lean 4`) checks only type syntax and inference rules. By design, the kernel cannot detect whether a submission sets the Navier–Stokes advection term to zero (`Nonlinear-Term Zero Degeneration`) or defines forcing by residual inversion at zero pressure ($p=0$, `Tautological Force Inversion` in `#376`), truncates an asymptotic complexity problem to a finite cutoff (`#378`), replaces 17 lines of an upstream author notice with 17 blank lines while placing 46 custom files inside an external namespace (`#005`), or writes one proof in the LaTeX manuscript (complex curve residues, ODE flows along vector fields, and Lazarsfeld's open cone citation) while formalizing a different algebraic mechanism in Lean (field extensions/traces, algebraic stalk Hasse–Taylor maps, and slope-ODE differential inequalities in `#017`).
2. **Capacity Limits of Manual Peer Review Under Multi-Million-Line Volume**:
   While human referees require months to verify a single 20-to-30-page manuscript, industrial AI pipelines deploy parallel worker swarms (`W06`~`W64`) to generate 95,000 to 7,528,000 lines of proof code per corpus.
3. **Need for Triple-Intersection Auditing (`Pure Mathematics` $\cap$ `Formal Kernel Engineering` $\cap$ `Large-Scale Static Analysis`)**:
   Auditing these artifacts requires simultaneously reading algebraic geometry / analytic number theory proofs, tracing `Lean 4` import graphs and `.patch` external-namespace placements, inspecting machine-code TCB hook bypasses (85.94%), and orchestrating parallel static/mutation verification pipelines.
4. **Institutional Independence**:
   Because many academic computer science laboratories depend on compute credits or sponsored grants from frontier AI corporations, an independent body is required to conduct byte-level forensic audits of public formal verification claims.

Consequently, **industrialized AI proof generation (`Swarm`) requires industrialized proof forensics (`Industrialized Proof Forensics`)**. This Charter establishes that verification standard.
