### 1.1 Mean-Square Formula and the Hardcoded $\sigma = 1/10$ Chain

In `preprints/The-Quasi-Riemann-Hypothesis-October-5-2026/build/paper2.tex` (L680–724, `openai/math` commit `4eaee87` / `PR #70` `ca28f20`), Proposition 2.1 establishes the mean-square estimate for the sextic-twisted Eisenstein Möbius sum $A_u(D)$ over all rows $0 < \mathcal{N}(u) \le H = D^{1+\vartheta}$:
$$\sum_{0 < \mathcal{N}(u) \le D^{1+\vartheta}} \lvert A_u(D)\rvert^2 \ll D^{2+\vartheta+2\eta}.$$
To detect the untwisted sum $A_1(D)$, one uses the sixth-power reciprocity mask $\chi_I(b^6) = \mathbf{1}_{(b, I)=1}$ over an amplifier of norm $Y = H^{1/6} = D^{\alpha}$ with $\alpha = \frac{1+\vartheta}{6}$. Applying Cauchy-Schwarz over $b$ gives a numerator of size $\sqrt{Y \cdot D^{2+\vartheta+2\eta}} = D^{\frac{2+\vartheta+\alpha}{2} + \eta}$, and dividing by the amplifier mass $Y = D^\alpha$ yields ([thm_02_symbolic_sixth_power_cauchy_schwarz_exponent](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L44-L55)):
$$E_{\mathrm{CS}}(\vartheta) = \frac{2 + \vartheta - \alpha}{2} = \frac{2 + \vartheta - \frac{1+\vartheta}{6}}{2} = \frac{11 + 5\vartheta}{12}.$$

The standalone submodule `ShortDraft.DirichletTarget` stopped at $\text{Re}(s) > \frac{23}{24}$ through four linked parameter choices:
1. `CubicSieve/ClosedRecursion.lean:120` fixed `denominator = 1000` and $\text{reserve} = \frac{1}{20} = 50/1000$ in the quadratic-symbol descent step.
2. `MeanSquare/ThetaDensity.lean:93` (`HasInitialCanonicalDensityAtRadius.nonzero_source_bound` / `exists_C_theta_density`) consequently required `(hσ : (1 : ℝ)/20 < σ)`.
3. `Mellin/SixthPowerAverage.lean:331` (`outside_totient_moebius_bound`) chose $\sigma = \vartheta = 1/10 > 1/20$.
4. `PrimeRows/NormFiberExtraction.lean:343` (`exists_good_dyadic_scale_zpow`) then fixed $H = Z^{11/10}$ and $Y = Z^{11/60}$, obtaining (L370–406, [thm_01_shortdraft_fixed_reserve_and_23_24_exponent](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L25-L38)):
   $$\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{11}{60} + 1 + \frac{11}{10}\right) = \frac{137}{120}, \qquad \frac{137}{120} - \frac{11}{60} = \frac{115}{120} = \frac{23}{24} = \frac{11 + 5(1/10)}{12}.$$

## 2. Pillar 2: The Unimported 30-File Sharp Cubic Large Sieve (`OAI/NumberTheory/CubicGauss/`)

Inside `lean/OAI/NumberTheory/CubicGauss/` (`openai/math` commit `4eaee87` / `PR #70` `ca28f20`; 30 files, 6,252 lines, 0 sorry), `CubicGauss/Recurrence.lean:350–370` and `CubicGauss/LargeSieve.lean:15–190` unconditionally formalize Heath-Brown's iterative cubic large sieve bootstrap (`cubic_large_sieve`), yet neither `DirichletL/` (2,926 files) nor `CubicMoment/` (3,549 files) imports `CubicGauss` ([thm_15_unimported_cubic_gauss_vs_bundled_cubic_gram_gap](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L297-L311)).

- `sieveThreshold` and `sieveStep` ([thm_09_cubic_gauss_sieve_step_and_threshold_identities](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L173-L185)):
  $$\text{sieveThreshold}(\alpha) = \frac{6\alpha - 5}{3\alpha - 1}, \qquad \text{sieveStep}(\alpha) = \frac{6\alpha - 4}{3\alpha - 1}.$$
- Quadratic slack, Möbius conjugacy linearization, and valid asymptotic simplification ([thm_10_cubic_gauss_quadratic_slack_and_attracting_fixed_point](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L190-L209), [thm_11_mobius_conjugacy_exact_closed_form_solution](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L214-L232)):
  Under the Möbius transformation $u(\alpha) = \frac{\alpha - 4/3}{\alpha - 1}$, the nonlinear rational map linearizes with zero approximation error into:
  $$u(\text{sieveStep}(\alpha)) = \frac{2}{3}\,u(\alpha), \qquad \tilde{\alpha}_n = \frac{\frac{4}{3} - \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{n+1}}{1 - \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{n+1}} \xrightarrow{n\to\infty} \frac{4}{3},$$
  whereas `CubicGauss/LargeSieve.lean:75` uses the geometric-series majorization $\alpha_n = \frac{4}{3} + \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{n+1}$ ([thm_12_nonlinear_vs_linearized_sieve_iterates_comparison](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L237-L254)). Both sequences converge to the exact same asymptotic fixed point $\alpha^{\ast} = \frac{4}{3}$, so `LargeSieve.lean:75` is a valid simplification for obtaining the $4/3$ limit (differing only by a factor of $3$ in the finite-$n$ excess $\alpha_n - 4/3$). Combining $\alpha^{\ast} = 4/3$ with cubic reciprocity (`short_four_thirds`, [thm_13_short_four_thirds_and_reciprocity_symmetrization](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L260-L272), [thm_14_scalar_bounds_epsilon_and_mixed_exponents](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L278-L290)) yields the sharp $M + N + (MN)^{2/3}$ bound.
