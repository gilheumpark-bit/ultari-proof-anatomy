## 1. Pillar 1: How a Hardcoded `1/20` Reserve Cut the $11/12$ Zero-Free Strip in Half (`23/24`)
In `preprints/The-Quasi-Riemann-Hypothesis-October-5-2026/build/paper2.tex` (`L680–724`), Proposition 2.1 establishes the mean-square estimate for the sextic-twisted Eisenstein Möbius sum $A_u(D)$ over all rows $0 < \mathcal{N}(u) \le H = D^{1+\vartheta}$:
$$\sum_{0 < \mathcal{N}(u) \le D^{1+\vartheta}} |A_u(D)|^2 \ll D^{2+\vartheta+2\eta}.$$
To detect the untwisted sum $A_1(D)$, one exploits the sixth-power reciprocity mask $\chi_I(b^6) = \mathbf{1}_{(b, I)=1}$ over an amplifier of norm $Y = H^{1/6} = D^{\alpha}$ with $\alpha = \frac{1+\vartheta}{6}$. Applying Cauchy-Schwarz over $b$ gives a numerator of size $\sqrt{Y \cdot D^{2+\vartheta+2\eta}} = D^{\frac{2+\vartheta+\alpha}{2} + \eta}$, and dividing by the amplifier mass $Y = D^\alpha$ yields ([`thm_02_symbolic_sixth_power_cauchy_schwarz_exponent`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L38-L50)):
$$E_{\mathrm{CS}}(\vartheta) = \frac{2 + \vartheta - \alpha}{2} = \frac{2 + \vartheta - \frac{1+\vartheta}{6}}{2} = \frac{11 + 5\vartheta}{12}.$$

Why did OpenAI's upstream `DirichletL/RowCompletion/ZeroFreeRegion.lean:197–214` (`Arithmetic/EisensteinCoordinates.lean:253`) end up proving $\text{Re}(s) > \frac{23}{24}$ instead of $\frac{11}{12}$?
- In `DirichletL/MeanSquare/ThetaDensity.lean:93` (`HasInitialCanonicalDensityAtRadius.nonzero_source_bound`), the hypothesis `(hσ : (1 : ℝ)/20 < σ)` was hardcoded because `CubicSieve/ClosedRecursion.lean:120` fixed the reciprocal step at `1/1000` and the initial reserve at `1/20 = 50/1000`.
- Forced to pick $\sigma = \vartheta > 1/20$, `DirichletL/Mellin/SixthPowerAverage.lean:331` (`outside_totient_moebius_bound`) hardcoded $\vartheta = 1/10$, setting $Y = Z^{11/60}$ (`L349`) and obtaining (`L370–406`, [`thm_01_shortdraft_fixed_reserve_and_23_24_exponent`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L19-L33)):
  $$\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{11}{60} + 1 + \frac{11}{10}\right) = \frac{137}{120}, \qquad \frac{137}{120} - \frac{11}{60} = \frac{115}{120} = \frac{23}{24} = \frac{11 + 5(1/10)}{12}.$$
- In `AlexKontorovich/quasi-rh`, replacing the hardcoded `(1000, 1/20)` pair with the typeclass `CanonicalDescentScale` (`50 ≤ reserve * denominator`, choosing $\kappa = \vartheta/2$ and $\text{denominator} = \lceil 100/\vartheta \rceil + 1$ in [`thm_04_canonical_descent_scale_budget_unlocks_arbitrary_theta`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L70-L85)) unlocks arbitrary $\vartheta > 0$. Setting $\vartheta = \frac{6\varepsilon}{5}$ and $\eta = \frac{\varepsilon}{2}$ gives $\frac{11 + 5\vartheta}{12} + \eta = \frac{11}{12} + \varepsilon$ ([`thm_03_kontorovich_epsilon_split_exact_11_12`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L54-L64)), while the remainder exponent $E_{\mathrm{rem}}(\vartheta) = \frac{5-\vartheta}{6}$ stays strictly subdominant ([`thm_05_remainder_exponent_subdominance_and_crossover`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L90-L103)).
- Furthermore, averaging over **all** Eisenstein integers $b$ in a smooth annulus (`Mellin/SixthPowerAverage.lean`, [`thm_06_sixth_power_fiber_cauchy_constant_768`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L108-L119), [`thm_07_totient_euler_correction_vs_prime_amplification_tradeoff`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L124-L134), [`thm_08_part_one_paper_tex_table_vs_shortdraft_gap`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L140-L154)) produces the totient weight $\prod_{\mathfrak{p}\mid I}(1 - \mathcal{N}(\mathfrak{p})^{-1})$ via radial Poisson summation, completely bypassing Landau's Prime Ideal Theorem ($J \asymp Y/\log Y$).

## 2. Pillar 2: The Unimported 30-File Sharp Cubic Large Sieve (`OAI/NumberTheory/CubicGauss/`)

- In [`thm_10_cubic_gauss_quadratic_slack_and_attracting_fixed_point`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L182-L202) and [`thm_11_mobius_conjugacy_exact_closed_form_solution`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L206-L224), we prove that under the Möbius transformation $u(\alpha) = \frac{\alpha - 4/3}{\alpha - 1}$, this nonlinear rational map linearizes **with zero error** into:

Combining $\alpha^* = 4/3$ with cubic reciprocity (`short_four_thirds`, [`thm_13_short_four_thirds_and_reciprocity_symmetrization`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L252-L265), [`thm_14_scalar_bounds_epsilon_and_mixed_exponents`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L270-L284)) yields the sharp $M + N + (MN)^{2/3}$ bound.
