## 1. Beyond Local Margin Checks: Five Structural Mysteries in the QRH Ecosystem
In [`Case 001`](../../001-openai-003-qrh/README.md) and [`Case 005`](../../005-openai-003-qrh-mathematician-referee-audit/README.md), we audited the constant-margin tables and unbundled the cubic Gram operator inside OpenAI's $\text{Re}(s) > 7/8 = 0.875$ proof (`lean/OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/`). 1. **Why does OpenAI's preliminary Lean spine (`ShortDraft`) prove $\text{Re}(s) > 23/24 \approx 0.9583$ when the papers claim $\text{Re}(s) > 11/12 \approx 0.9167$?**

   - In [`thm_01_shortdraft_fixed_reserve_and_23_24_exponent`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L19-L33) and [`thm_04_canonical_descent_scale_budget_unlocks_arbitrary_theta`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L70-L85), we prove that this happened because a single descent parameter (`reserve = 1/20`, `step = 1/1000`) was hardcoded as a constant instead of a symbolic variable, forcing the mean-square radius $\vartheta$ to be fixed at $1/10$ (`(11 + 5*(1/10))/12 = 23/24`).

2. **Why is a complete 30-file, 6,252-line proof of Heath-Brown's Sharp Cubic Large Sieve (`CubicGauss/`) sitting completely unimported?**

   - Yet **zero files** in `DirichletL` (`2,926` files) or `CubicMoment` (`3,549` files) import `CubicGauss` ([`thm_15_unimported_cubic_gauss_vs_bundled_cubic_gram_gap`](../proofs/qrh_eleven_twelfths_and_cubic_large_sieve.lean#L289-L303))! Instead, the $7/8$ proof imports a weaker one-step bundled bound (`CubicGram`), leaving major exponent savings on the table.
   - Moreover, by defining the recursive nonlinear sieve sequence `sieveIter` in Lean 4 and applying mathematical induction (`induction n`) in [`thm_31_sieve_iter_mobius_invariants_by_induction`](../proofs/qrh_trilogy_induction_and_algebraic_bridge.lean#L36-L69) through [`thm_33_sieve_iter_strictly_beats_openai_sieve_exponent_all_n`](../proofs/qrh_trilogy_induction_and_algebraic_bridge.lean#L92-L107), we prove the exact closed-form solution $\tilde{\alpha}_n = \frac{4\cdot 3^n - 2^{n+1}}{3\cdot 3^n - 2^{n+1}}$ for **all** $n \in \mathbb{N}$.
