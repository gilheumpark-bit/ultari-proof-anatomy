### 2.1 Cube Floor Margin Root (`37/72 ≈ 0.513889`) & Parameter Budget at `0.5001`

$$\operatorname{sourceExponent}\left(\frac{1}{2}+\eta, \frac{13}{16}, 1, \eta\right) - \frac{3}{16} = \frac{3}{2}\eta - \frac{1}{48}$$

- At $\eta_{\max} = \frac{1}{72}$ ($a_{\max} = \frac{37}{72} \approx 0.513889$), the floor saving vanishes ([`Theorem 12`](../proofs/qrh_exponent_and_parameter_bounds.lean#L138-L139)).

### 2.2 Stationary Point (`0.693319`) vs. Rational SOS Reference Point (`79/204`)
In [`DirichletL/Endpoint.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/Endpoint.lean), at $y = 0$:
$$E_{\text{bal}}(\delta, 0) = \frac{1224\delta^2 - 948\delta + 185}{48(138\delta - 185)}$$
Solving $\frac{\partial E_{\text{bal}}}{\partial \delta}(\delta, 0) = 0$ yields $9384\delta^2 - 25160\delta + 8325 = 0$, whose unique root in $[0, 5/6]$ is:
$$\delta^* = \frac{185}{138} - \frac{35\sqrt{16354}}{4692} \approx 0.3866375622 \implies a^* = \frac{1+\delta^*}{2} = \frac{323}{276} - \frac{35\sqrt{16354}}{9384} \approx 0.6933187811$$
To avoid irrational square roots in Lean 4, OpenAI used the rational point $\delta_{\text{SOS}} = \frac{79}{204}$ ($a_{\text{SOS}} = \frac{283}{408} \approx 0.69362745$), where $(4 \cdot 51 \cdot \delta - 79)^2 = 0$, $E_{\text{bal}}\left(\frac{79}{204}, 0\right) = -\frac{7}{30672}$, $\frac{\partial^2 E_{\text{bal}}}{\partial \delta^2}\left(\frac{79}{204}, 0\right) = -\frac{236315300}{608806611} < 0$, and $\frac{\partial E_{\text{bal}}}{\partial y}\left(\frac{79}{204}, 0\right) = -\frac{2933665}{333189936} < 0$ ([`Theorem 4`](../proofs/qrh_exponent_and_parameter_bounds.lean#L86-L91), [`Theorem 5`](../proofs/qrh_exponent_and_parameter_bounds.lean#L95-L99), [`Theorem 27`](../proofs/qrh_exponent_and_parameter_bounds.lean#L386-L402)).
