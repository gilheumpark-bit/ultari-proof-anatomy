Checking that a Lean 4 file compiles without syntax errors is only the starting point. When an analytic number theorist or journal referee examines OpenAI `#003` (*Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis at $\beta = 7/8$ and Siegel Zero Exclusion*), they ask five deeper questions:
1. **Where did the hardcoded constants come from?**
2. **How much precision was thrown away for coding convenience, and how do the modules connect?**

5. **What is the conditional transfer blueprint from $\beta = 0.69$ to the critical line $\beta = 1/2$?**

## 1. Question 1: Where Did the Hardcoded Constants Come From? (Key Trick Extraction)

Opening [`Parameters.lean:6-10`](proofs/oai_ref/Parameters.lean#L6-L10), one immediately sees three unexplained fractions:
* $\alpha_X = 17/48$, $\alpha_Y = 23/48$, $\ell = 1/6$

Reverse-engineering the proof shows that these three fractions are the **unique solution to a $3 \times 3$ linear system** balancing three competing forces ([`thm_01_magic_exponents_unique_3x3_system`](proofs/qrh_mathematician_referee_audit.lean#L16-L23)):
1. **Contour Anchor ($\ell = 1/6 = 8/48$)**: The main Mellin contour is placed at $\mathrm{Re}(z) = 1/6$.
2. **Square-Full Exceptional Shift ($\alpha_X - \ell = 3/16 = 9/48$)**: Controlling square-full moduli forces $\alpha_X = 8/48 + 9/48 = 17/48$.
3. **Cubic Gauss $P^{1/6}$ Cancellation ($(5\alpha_X + \alpha_Y)/12 = 3/16$)**: Matching the cubic Gauss sum $L^2$-saving to $3/16$ forces $5(17/48) + \alpha_Y = 108/48 \implies \alpha_Y = 23/48$.

Similarly, in the Siegel-zero module ([`GeometricBoundary.lean:26`](proofs/oai_ref/GeometricBoundary.lean#L26), [`PivotBounds.lean:63-65`](proofs/oai_ref/PivotBounds.lean#L63-L65)), the integer constant **$H = 86,713,344$** and threshold **$N_0 = 18,818$** come from the floor bound $\lfloor 96t \rfloor + 1 \le 97t$ ($97 = 96 + 1$, [`thm_21_siegel_zero_magic_constants_86713344_and_18818`](proofs/qrh_mathematician_referee_audit.lean#L257-L265)):
* From $Q \le 97^2 t^2$, the dimension bound `twice_pivot_floor_square_le` sets $N_0 = 2 \times 97^2 = 18,818$ while the $M^2/(4Q)$ lower bound independently sets $1/c_1 = 4 \times 97^2 = 37,636$ (sharing the common floor factor $97^2 = 9,409$).
* With upper constant $C_1 = 2 \times 96 = 192$, choosing $H = 12 \times (C_1 / c_1) = 12 \times 192 \times 37,636 = 86,713,344$ makes the ratio $(C_1/c_1)/H$ equal to $1/12$.

## 3. Question 3 & 4: Why Did It Stop at β = 7/8, and How Do the Edge Cases Work?

1. **Double Contour Separation**: On $w = 19/20$, $\mathrm{Re}(z) = 1/6 + e$ clears the pole at $59/48$ by $13/240 > 0$. On the $w = 1$ residue, $\mathrm{Re}(z) = 1/6$ would cross the pole ($-1/16 < 0$), so the contour shifts left to **$\mathrm{Re}(z) = 33/200 < 1/6$**, clearing the pole at $65/48$ by $29/1200 > 0$ ([`thm_14_double_contour_w_and_z_residue_separation_budget`](proofs/qrh_mathematician_referee_audit.lean#L178-L186)).
2. **Lowering Below $\beta = 7/8$**: Achieving $\beta = 5/6$, $3/4$, or $69/100$ requires off-diagonal cubic Gauss Gram savings of $\Delta_{\mathrm{gram}} = 1/16$, $3/16$, and $111/400$, respectively ([`thm_16_bilinear_gram_cancellation_thresholds_5_6_and_3_4`](proofs/qrh_mathematician_referee_audit.lean#L199-L206)).
3. **Bad Primes $Q = 3, 4$ in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$**: $Q = 3$ is wildly ramified ($\lambda = 1-\omega \mid 3$) and $Q = 4$ ($p=2$ inert) collapses sextic characters into $\mathbb{F}_4^\times \cong C_3$; `badPrimeMask` removes $p \mid 6$ so all remaining prime powers $Q \ge 7$ satisfy $Q \equiv 1 \pmod 6$ ([`thm_19_eisenstein_sextic_symbol_requires_Q_ge_7_mod_6_eq_1`](proofs/qrh_mathematician_referee_audit.lean#L236-L242)).
4. **Real Quadratic vs. Complex Cubic Characters**: Siegel zeros only arise for real quadratic characters ($\chi^2 = \chi_0, \bar{\chi} = \chi$), treated via biquadratic Klein 4-group torus jets $B = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{d}, \sqrt{2})$ ([`thm_22_biquadratic_klein4_torus_jet_exponent_gap`](proofs/qrh_mathematician_referee_audit.lean#L268-L274)), whereas pure cubic characters ($\chi^3 = \chi_0, \bar{\chi} = \chi^2 \neq \chi$) have no Siegel zeros ([`thm_23_siegel_zero_conditional_hgeometry_vs_cubic_independence`](proofs/qrh_mathematician_referee_audit.lean#L278-L284)).
