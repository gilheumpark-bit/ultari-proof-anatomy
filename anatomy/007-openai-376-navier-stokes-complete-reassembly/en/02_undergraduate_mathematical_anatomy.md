## 2. Breakthrough #1: Non-Zero Pressure Solenoidal Force ($\operatorname{div} f_{\text{sol}} = 0$) and 4.53e7x Tightening of the Screened Green's Operator

### 2.1 Two-Mode Velocity Ansatz and Exact Pressure Poisson Solution (PeriodicLattice/Solenoidal.lean)

On $\mathbb{T}^3 = (\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z})^3$, setting
$$\eta(z) = -\frac{\sin(2\pi z)}{2\pi}, \qquad \text{slope}(z) = \eta'(z) = -\cos(2\pi z), \qquad \text{doubleSlope}(z) = 2\text{slope}(z)^2 - 1 = \cos(4\pi z)$$
yields the exact identities ([Solenoidal.lean:41-56](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Analysis/PeriodicLattice/Solenoidal.lean#L41-L56)):
$$\text{slope}(z)^2 + (2\pi)^2 \eta(z)^2 = 1, \qquad \text{doubleSlope}''(z) = -(4\pi)^2 \text{doubleSlope}(z).$$

For the divergence-free velocity field $u(t,x,y,z) = \big(\text{slope}(z)\,a(t,y),\; \text{slope}(z)\,b(t,y),\; -\eta(z)\,\partial_y b(t,y)\big)$, the divergence of the raw residual $g = \partial_t u - \nu \Delta u + (u\cdot\nabla)u$ decomposes into two vertical harmonics ($k_z = 0$ and $k_z = 2$, [thm_04_solenoidal_convective_divergence_two_mode_split](../proofs/ns376_unimported_solenoidal_and_leray_bounds.lean#L62-L70)):
$$\operatorname{div} g(t,y,z) = \partial_y^2 \underbrace{\left(\frac{1}{2}\big(b(t,y)^2 - \overline{b^2}(t)\big)\right)}_{\text{pressureBase}(t,y)} + \text{doubleSlope}(z)\,\underbrace{\big((\partial_y b)^2 - b\,\partial_y^2 b\big)}_{\text{pressureSource}(t,y)}.$$
Applying the 1D screened inverse operator $\mathcal{S}_{4\pi} = (\partial_y^2 - (4\pi)^2)^{-1}$ to define $\text{pressureMode} := \mathcal{S}_{4\pi}(\text{pressureSource})$ and setting $\phi = \text{pressureBase} + \text{doubleSlope}(z)\,\text{pressureMode}$ gives $\Delta \phi = \operatorname{div} g$ identically ([thm_05_solenoidal_nonzero_pressure_poisson_cancellation](../proofs/ns376_unimported_solenoidal_and_leray_bounds.lean#L74-L80)). Thus $p := -\phi \neq 0$ and $f_{\text{sol}} := g - \nabla \phi$ satisfy $\operatorname{div} f_{\text{sol}} = 0$ and the full incompressible Navier-Stokes equations ([Solenoidal.lean:334-373](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Analysis/PeriodicLattice/Solenoidal.lean#L334-L373)).

### 2.2 Recalculating PeriodicInverse.lean: From $286,753.3$ to $\frac{1}{16\pi^2} \approx 0.006333$ (45,282,268x Tightening)

In [PeriodicInverse.lean:183,328,340](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Analysis/PeriodicLattice/PeriodicInverse.lean#L183-L345), OpenAI defines $\mathcal{S}_\lambda H = \mathcal{I}_{-\lambda}(\mathcal{I}_\lambda H)$ via $(\mathcal{I}_\lambda H)(t,y) = (1 - e^\lambda)^{-1} \int_0^1 e^{\lambda s} H(t, y-s)\,ds$ ([thm_06_periodic_inverse_screened_helmholtz_factorization](../proofs/ns376_unimported_solenoidal_and_leray_bounds.lean#L85-L92)), and bounds $e^{\lambda s} \le e^{|\lambda|}$ pointwise on $[0,1]$.
