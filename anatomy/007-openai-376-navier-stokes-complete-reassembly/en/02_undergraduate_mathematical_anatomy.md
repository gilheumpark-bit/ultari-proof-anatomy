## 2. Breakthrough #1: Non-Zero Pressure Solenoidal Force ($\operatorname{div} f_{\text{sol}} = 0$) and `4.53e7x` Tightening of the Screened Green's Operator

### 2.1 Two-Mode Velocity Ansatz and Exact Pressure Poisson Solution (`PeriodicLattice/Solenoidal.lean`)

On $\mathbb{T}^3 = (\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z})^3$, setting
$$\eta(z) = -\frac{\sin(2\pi z)}{2\pi}, \qquad \text{slope}(z) = \eta'(z) = -\cos(2\pi z), \qquad \text{doubleSlope}(z) = 2\text{slope}(z)^2 - 1 = \cos(4\pi z)$$
yields the exact identities ([`Solenoidal.lean:41-56`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Analysis/PeriodicLattice/Solenoidal.lean#L41-L56)):
$$\text{slope}(z)^2 + (2\pi)^2 \eta(z)^2 = 1, \qquad \text{doubleSlope}''(z) = -(4\pi)^2 \text{doubleSlope}(z).$$

For the divergence-free velocity field $u(t,x,y,z) = \big(\text{slope}(z)\,a(t,y),\; \text{slope}(z)\,b(t,y),\; -\eta(z)\,\partial_y b(t,y)\big)$, the divergence of the raw residual $g = \partial_t u - \nu \Delta u + (u\cdot\nabla)u$ decomposes into two vertical harmonics ($k_z = 0$ and $k_z = 2$, [`thm_04_solenoidal_convective_divergence_two_mode_split`](../proofs/ns376_unimported_solenoidal_and_leray_bounds.lean#L62-L80)):
$$\operatorname{div} g(t,y,z) = \partial_y^2 \underbrace{\left(\frac{1}{2}\big(b(t,y)^2 - \overline{b^2}(t)\big)\right)}_{\text{pressureBase}(t,y)} + \text{doubleSlope}(z)\,\underbrace{\big((\partial_y b)^2 - b\,\partial_y^2 b\big)}_{\text{pressureSource}(t,y)}.$$
Applying the 1D screened inverse operator $\mathcal{S}_{4\pi} = (\partial_y^2 - (4\pi)^2)^{-1}$ to define $\text{pressureMode} := \mathcal{S}_{4\pi}(\text{pressureSource})$ and setting $\phi = \text{pressureBase} + \text{doubleSlope}(z)\,\text{pressureMode}$ gives $\Delta \phi = \operatorname{div} g$ identically ([`thm_05_solenoidal_nonzero_pressure_poisson_cancellation`](../proofs/ns376_unimported_solenoidal_and_leray_bounds.lean#L74-L92)). Thus $p := -\phi \neq 0$ and $f_{\text{sol}} := g - \nabla \phi$ satisfy $\operatorname{div} f_{\text{sol}} = 0$ and the full incompressible Navier-Stokes equations ([`Solenoidal.lean:315-366`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Analysis/PeriodicLattice/Solenoidal.lean#L315-L366)).

### 2.2 Recalculating `PeriodicInverse.lean`: From $286,753.3$ to $\frac{1}{16\pi^2} \approx 0.006333$ (`45,282,268x` Tightening)

In [`PeriodicInverse.lean:183,328,340`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Analysis/PeriodicLattice/PeriodicInverse.lean#L183-L345), OpenAI defines $\mathcal{S}_\lambda H = \mathcal{I}_{-\lambda}(\mathcal{I}_\lambda H)$ via $(\mathcal{I}_\lambda H)(t,y) = (1 - e^\lambda)^{-1} \int_0^1 e^{\lambda s} H(t, y-s)\,ds$ ([`thm_06_periodic_inverse_screened_helmholtz_factorization`](../proofs/ns376_unimported_solenoidal_and_leray_bounds.lean#L85-L107)), and bounds $e^{\lambda s} \le e^{|\lambda|}$ pointwise on $[0,1]$. At $\lambda = 4\pi$, this multiplies the bound of $\mathcal{I}_{-4\pi}$ by a spurious factor $e^{|-4\pi|} = e^{4\pi} \approx 286,751.31$ even though $e^{-4\pi s} \le 1$ on $[0,1]$, producing the factor $K_{\text{OpenAI}}(4\pi) = \frac{e^{12\pi}}{(e^{4\pi}-1)^2} \approx 286,753.3131$.

Integrating the positive kernel $e^{\pm \lambda s} > 0$ directly on $[0,1]$ yields
$$\|\mathcal{I}_{\pm \lambda}\|_{L^\infty \to L^\infty} = |1 - e^{\pm \lambda}|^{-1} \int_0^1 e^{\pm \lambda s}\,ds = \frac{|e^{\pm \lambda} - 1|}{|\lambda|\cdot |1 - e^{\pm \lambda}|} = \frac{1}{|\lambda|},$$
so the exact operator norm of $\mathcal{S}_{4\pi}$ is $\|\mathcal{S}_{4\pi}\|_{L^\infty \to L^\infty} = \frac{1}{16\pi^2} \approx 0.006332574$, which is **$16\pi^2 \frac{e^{12\pi}}{(e^{4\pi}-1)^2} \approx \mathbf{45,282,268.18}$ times tighter** than OpenAI's bound ([`thm_07`–`thm_10`](../proofs/ns376_unimported_solenoidal_and_leray_bounds.lean#L97-L198)).
