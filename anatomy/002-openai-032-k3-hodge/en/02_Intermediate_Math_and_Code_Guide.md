## 1. The Symplectic Double-Bind Relation

In real source dimension $n = 2k$ ($k = 4$), `topology.tex:557` locks the intersection sign of every graded Lagrangian double point $x$ to the parity of its Maslov branch degree $\lvert x\rvert$:

$$\operatorname{sign}(x) = (-1)^k (-1)^{\lvert x\rvert}$$

- Topological cancellation (`topology.tex`) requires positive sign $+(-1)^k$, forcing $\lvert x\rvert$ to be even ([odd_degree_trace_sign_always_opposite](../proofs/k3_forensic_paths.lean#L36-L48), [eliashberg_murphy_cancellation_fails](../proofs/k3_forensic_paths.lean#L54-L59)).
- Curvature vanishing (`curvature.tex:159, 459, 533`) requires avoiding degree $\lvert x\rvert \in \{2, 2k-2\}$, which fails at the non-loose unknot $\phi_0$ because the forward stabilization trace $G_1 : \phi_0 \to \phi_1$ produces $(\lvert x_{12}\rvert, \lvert x_{21}\rvert) = (2, 2k-2)$ ([eems_poincare_hopf_and_maslov_degrees](../proofs/k3_forensic_paths.lean#L154-L171)). This parity barrier explains why the three non-CM manuscripts had to be retracted by structural necessity and gives the exact reason why no local stabilization can simultaneously cancel self-intersections and preserve $m_0 = 0$.
