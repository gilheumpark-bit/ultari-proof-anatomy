## 1. Executive Summary

OpenAI's flagship number-theory family **`#005` (`Catalan's constant is irrational`, September 24, 2026)** proves that Catalan's constant
$$G = \beta(2) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n}{(2n+1)^2} \approx 0.915965594177\dots$$
is irrational: **`theorem catalan_irrational : Irrational (∑' j : ℕ, (-1 : ℝ) ^ j / ((2 * j + 1 : ℕ) : ℝ) ^ 2)`** (`change Irrational catalan`; [`main.tex`](../proofs/oai_ref/tex/main.tex#L1-L44), [`introduction.tex`](../proofs/oai_ref/tex/introduction.tex#L1-L55), [`Challenge_Catalan.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/Challenge_Catalan.lean#L7-L9), [`Conclusions.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/Conclusions.lean#L11-L24), [`Catalan.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/Catalan.lean#L1-L11), [`Main.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/Main.lean#L1-L30)). Introduced by Eugène Charles Catalan in 1865, $G$ had resisted all classical Padé and hypergeometric irrationality proofs (despite Apéry's 1979 proof for $\zeta(3)$ and Zudilin's 2003 well-poised hypergeometric method (`Zudilin2003`)) because single-column hypergeometric approximations to $G$ have denominator growth `lcm(1..n)^2 ~ e^{2n}` that exceeds their real-place decay.

Our forensic anatomy dissects all **`981` Lean 4 files (`573,816` lines)** in `OAI/NumberTheory/Catalan/` (`982` files / `573,829` lines including `ComparatorChallenges/Catalan.lean`), all **`9` LaTeX manuscript files (`3,001` lines)**, and **`28` BibTeX entries (`319` lines)** in [`references.bib`](../proofs/oai_ref/tex/references.bib#L1-L319), backed by **`75` standalone machine-checked Lean 4 theorems (`1,313` lines, `0 sorry`, `432/432` hypothesis mutations caught, `69/75` structural, `12` nonlinear/inductive proofs)** and deterministic symbolic verification ([`verify_symbolic_catalan005.py`](../proofs/verify_symbolic_catalan005.py#L1-L631)).

## 2. The Five Causal `Why`s Behind the Proof Architecture

### [Why 1] Why Do Classical Single-Column Approximants Fail for $G$, and How Does the $n \times n$ ($n = 48N$) Chebyshev-Laurent Determinant Eliminate $\zeta(2)$?
- Expanding the complete elliptic integral $f(t) = \frac{1}{\pi}\int_0^1 \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x(1-x)(1-tx)}} = \sum_{j=0}^\infty \binom{2j}{j}^2 \frac{t^j}{16^j}$ produces linear combinations of **two** periods: $G$ and $\frac{3}{2}\zeta(2) = \frac{\pi^2}{4}$ ([`foundations.tex`](../proofs/oai_ref/tex/foundations.tex#L113-L148)).
- To eliminate $\zeta(2)$ without losing control of denominators, the proof builds an $n \times n$ determinant $\Delta_N$ at scale $n = 48N$ ($a = 11N, b = 7N, q = g = 4N, h = 2N, L = 59N, C = 63N, H = 65N, A = 19N$; [`thm_01_catalan_row_parameter_scaling_hierarchy`](../proofs/catalan_rows_two_adic_and_odd_primes.lean#L20-L34)) from Chebyshev-Laurent rows $P_r(t) = (1-t)^h t^{C-1} T_{|r-g|}(1/t)$ ([`FixedCompactRowsAll.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/FixedCompactRowsAll.lean#L1-L125), [`thm_02_chebyshev_row_degree_support_window`](../proofs/catalan_rows_two_adic_and_odd_primes.lean#L40-L51)).
- Because $t P_r(t)/f(t) - D_r(t) = O(t^{C + r - g})$ vanishes to order $\ge C - g = 59N = L$ at $t = 0$, every filtered raw column index $b \le j < L$ ($7N \le j < 59N$) has **zero** $\zeta(2)$ coefficient (`[t^j](t P_r/f - D_r) = 0`), leaving entries of the form $a_{r,k} G - b_{r,k}$ that depend strictly on $G$ ([`thm_03_taylor_contact_order_cancels_zeta_two`](../proofs/catalan_rows_two_adic_and_odd_primes.lean#L57-L68), [`thm_04_integer_column_filter_support_in_contact_window`](../proofs/catalan_rows_two_adic_and_odd_primes.lean#L74-L85)).

### [Why 2] Why Does the Finite-Place Denominator Growth Drop From $2n^2$ to $(2.29084 - o(1))n^2$?
- At the **2-adic place** ($p = 2$), clearing powers of $2$ across the $n$ columns reduces via Cauchy–Binet to minimizing a quadratic form $Q(m,n) = 2(96m - 53n)^2 - 505n^2 \ge -505n^2$ in homogeneous scale $48n$, yielding a 2-adic exponent contribution of $-\left(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{505}{4608}\right)\log 2 = -\frac{2809}{4608}\log 2 \approx -0.422525$ ([`foundations.tex`](../proofs/oai_ref/tex/foundations.tex#L415-L434), [`thm_06_two_adic_quadratic_completion_of_square`](../proofs/catalan_rows_two_adic_and_odd_primes.lean#L106-L117)).
- At **odd primes** $2\sqrt{H} < p \le H$, Kummer's base-$p$ carry theorem ([`odd-primes.tex`](../proofs/oai_ref/tex/odd-primes.tex#L77-L88), [`thm_09_kummer_odd_digit_single_carry_identity`](../proofs/catalan_rows_two_adic_and_odd_primes.lean#L154-L164)) and local column elimination ([`OddLowerPairPool.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OddLowerPairPool.lean#L215-L260), [`OddPrimeCentralZetaContraction.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OddPrimeCentralZetaContraction.lean#L340-L380)) compress the $p$-adic valuation loss into an 8-piece piecewise-linear profile $d(x)$ on $[0, 65]$ with exact rational integral $\int_0^{65} d(x)\,dx = \frac{8609}{2}$ ([`thm_15_eight_piece_odd_loss_integral_8609_halves`](../proofs/catalan_rows_two_adic_and_odd_primes.lean#L262-L274)).
- Dividing by $48^2 = 2304$ via the weighted Prime Number Theorem ([`ContinuousWeightedPNT.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/ContinuousWeightedPNT.lean#L1-L134), [`ImportedPNTCoreBridge.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/ImportedPNTCoreBridge.lean#L1-L43), [`FinitePlaceAssembly.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/FinitePlaceAssembly.lean#L1485-L1514), [`FinitePlaceNumerics.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/FinitePlaceNumerics.lean#L13-L31)) gives the total finite-place valuation bound $-\frac{8609}{4608} - \frac{2809}{4608}\log 2 \approx -2.2908107 > -2.29084$ ([`thm_16_finite_place_rational_lower_bound_certificate`](../proofs/catalan_rows_two_adic_and_odd_primes.lean#L282-L293)).
