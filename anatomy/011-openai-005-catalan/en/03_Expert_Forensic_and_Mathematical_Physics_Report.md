1. **Beyond-Baseline Alternative Solution: One-Shot $\mathbb{F}_{101}$ Right-Inverse Certificate vs `376,998`-Line Step-by-Step $\mathbb{F}_{101}$ Gaussian Trace**:

   - **Step 3 (Derivation)**: Because every prime factor of $S_{\text{scale}}$ is $\le 97 < 101$ ($S_{\text{scale}} \equiv 62 \not\equiv 0 \pmod{101}$; [`thm_42_scaling_constant_64_digit_prime_support_below_101`](../proofs/catalan_palindromic_frobenius_and_modular.lean#L310-L320)), modular reduction of $\mathcal{B}_0 + \sigma\mathcal{B}_1$ ($\sigma \in \{0, 1, -1\}$) yields
