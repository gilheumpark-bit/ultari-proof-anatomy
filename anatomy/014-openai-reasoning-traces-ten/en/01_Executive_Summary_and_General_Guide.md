## 2. Five Core Questions (`Q1` to `Q5`)

### Q1 (Compiler & Provenance Evidence): How do we know the `10` PDFs are post-hoc compiled summaries rather than raw model transcripts?
Every trace header explicitly labels its body `Summarized chain of thought (...)`, and in `9` of the `10` cases OpenAI deleted the `.tex` source used to build the PDF before commit `adc7f124`. Inside `reasoning_traces/`, however, the `211`-line LuaLaTeX source [`mezard-parisi-formula.tex`](../proofs/oai_ref/preprints_tex/mezard-parisi-formula.tex#L1-L211) (`#221`) was left behind. That file defines five complexity macros `\UGC`, `\CSP`, `\NP`, `\OPT`, `\SDP` (`L47-L56`) belonging to `#102` (*Unique Games*) rather than spin glasses, loads `FandolFang-Regular.otf` (`L68`), and wires two active CJK characters `自` (`U+81EA`) and `由` (`U+7531`, `"自由"`) into `\QuoteGlyphSetup` (`L76-L78`) and `CoTQuote` (`L79`). Because neither character appears in the body (`L82-L211`) and `0 / 10` released PDFs embed `FandolFang` (`pdffonts` check), this CJK hook is a shared wrapper fallback guarding against raw multilingual CoT tokens (such as `"自由群"` or `"自由能"`). By contrast, `#221`'s actual manuscript [`glyph-mappings.tex`](../proofs/oai_ref/preprints_tex/glyph-mappings.tex#L1-L40) maps `38` mathematical delimiter/operator glyphs (`L2-L39`) and `0` CJK characters, while `mezard-parisi-formula.tex` `L154` inserts `[Administrative details redacted.]`.

### Q2 (Bidirectional Citation Replacement): Why did `69.28%` (`230 / 332`) of the literature cited in the reasoning logs disappear from the primary papers?
During problem solving, the reasoning traces explicitly list `332` numbered `References` (`5` in `#221` up to `67` in `#087` and `56` in `#102`) used for reductions, parameter schedules, and dead-end checks. While pruning dead-end search references during paper drafting is routine in mathematical exposition, two quantitative patterns go well beyond dead-end cleanup. First, across the `11` primary manuscripts (`315` bibliography entries), only `102` trace references survive (`230` dropped = `69.28%`) while **`213` post-hoc entries (`67.62%`)** absent from the traces are added, reducing the trace-to-paper Jaccard overlap across `545` distinct citation slots to `18.72%` (`#287` drops `49 / 54 = 90.7%`). Second, `#017` cites two OpenAI 2026 companion papers (`[17]` *Catalan's constant is irrational*, cited over `20` times in Part I, and `[18]` *Joint Dickman law*) and `#159` cites `[46]` (*Monochromatic finite sums and products*); **all `3 / 3` (`100%`) internal companion citations were omitted** from the published bibliographies.

### Q3 (Mathematical Pivots Across `10` Domains): What actual mathematical engines make the `10` proofs work?
Beneath the post-hoc packaging, the `10` traces contain substantive algebraic, combinatorial, and analytic pivots:
1. **`#017` ($\mu(\pi) = 2$)**: Across `39` pages, Part I pushes Zeilberger-Zudilin hypergeometric weights from `7.1032...` down to `62/25 = 2.48 < 5/2`, which **already establishes** Flint-Hills series convergence ($2(62/25) - 5 = -1/25 < 0$), but hits the single-box rational Dirichlet barrier $1 + 1/\sqrt{a} > 2$ short of $\mu(\pi) = 2$. ### Q4 (Three-Stage Scope Attrition): How much shrinks between the `22` manuscripts, the `10` traces, and the `21` Lean comparator files?

### Q5 (Verification Boundary): Why do surface stub scanners misjudge `ComparatorChallenges/*.lean` while missing `2,170` real `OAI/` defects?
Every file in `ComparatorChallenges/*.lean` ends with `:= by sorry` because it specifies only the target proposition. A naive syntax scanner that checks whether hypothesis binders (`hNP`, `hK`, `hM`) reappear literally in the conclusion type flags `5` false positives (`OrdinaryElliott.lean`, `MahlerConjecture.lean`, `SymmetricMahlerEquality.lean`, `SymmetricPolar.lean`, `DilutedSpin.lean`). Inspecting the actual `OAI/` proof terms (`BinaryCorrectedElliott.lean`, `MainTheorem.lean`, `Classification.lean`) proves **all `5 / 5` hypotheses ARE genuinely consumed**. Conversely, scanning all `9,929` internal `OAI/` proof files uncovers **`2,170` genuinely unused lemma hypotheses** (`1,537` concentrated in `#159`'s `4,774` `Progressions/` files), demonstrating why formal verification audits must inspect elaborated proof terms rather than `:= by sorry` stubs.
