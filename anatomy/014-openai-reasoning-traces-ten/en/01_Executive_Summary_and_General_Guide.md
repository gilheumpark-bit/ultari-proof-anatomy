## 2. Five Core Questions (Q1 to Q5)

### Q1 (Compiler & Provenance Evidence): How do we know the 10 PDFs are post-hoc compiled summaries rather than raw model transcripts?
Nine of the 10 trace PDFs (9/10; all except #221, which carries Original prompt (excerpts) directly without that subtitle line) label their body Summarized chain of thought (...), and in 9 of the 10 cases the .tex source used to build the PDF is absent at openai/math commit 4eaee87 (4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae). Inside reasoning_traces/, however, the 211-line LuaLaTeX source [mezard-parisi-formula.tex](../proofs/oai_ref/preprints_tex/mezard-parisi-formula.tex#L1-L211) (#221) was left behind. That file defines five complexity macros UGC, CSP, NP, OPT, SDP (L47-L56) belonging to #102 (Unique Games) rather than spin glasses, loads FandolFang-Regular.otf (L68), and wires two active CJK characters 自 (U+81EA) and 由 (U+7531, "自由") into QuoteGlyphSetup (L76-L78) and CoTQuote (L79). Because neither character appears in the body (L82-L211) and 0 / 10 released PDFs embed FandolFang (pdffonts check), this CJK hook is a shared wrapper fallback guarding against raw multilingual CoT tokens (such as "自由群" or "自由能"). By contrast, #221's actual manuscript [glyph-mappings.tex](../proofs/oai_ref/preprints_tex/glyph-mappings.tex#L1-L40) maps 38 mathematical delimiter/operator glyphs (L2-L39) and 0 CJK characters, while mezard-parisi-formula.tex L154 records [Administrative details redacted.] as a post-hoc administrative redaction marker (typesetting metadata).
