## 1. Executive Summary

The **dimension-six mutually unbiased bases (MUB)** problem (`Zauner1999`, `WoottersFields1989`, `Ivanovic1981`) asks for the maximum number $N(6)$ of orthonormal bases in $\mathbb{C}^6$ such that every pair of vectors $u, v$ from distinct bases satisfies $|\langle u, v \rangle| = 1/\sqrt{6}$. Whenever the dimension $d = p^m$ is a prime power, finite-field Weyl-Heisenberg phase spaces construct a complete family of $d + 1$ bases ($N(2)=3, N(3)=4, N(4)=5, N(5)=6$). At the smallest composite dimension $d = 6 = 2 \times 3$, no six-element finite field $\mathbb{F}_6$ exists, leaving $\min(N(2), N(3)) = 3 \le N(6) \le 7$ open for nearly five decades.

OpenAI's problem family **`#266` (`Mutually unbiased bases in dimension six`, September 24, 2026)** comprises two companion preprints together with the single largest formalization directory in `openai/math`: **`3,386` Lean 4 files (`2,835,031` lines)** in `OAI/Analysis/MutuallyUnbiased/` plus **`9` files (`1,329` lines)** in `OAI/LinearAlgebra/Hadamard/` ([`266.md`](../proofs/oai_ref/docs/266.md#L1-L23), [`MUBSix.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/challenges/MUBSix.lean#L1-L66), [`HadamardCubeFiber.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/challenges/HadamardCubeFiber.lean#L1-L37)). Our forensic audit establishes a decisive split: **Preprint 1's headline claim $N(6) = 3$ relies entirely on `100` native `binary64` floating-point C++ process runs and has `0` lines (`0%`) of Lean 4 formalization**, whereas the Lean 4 repository formalizes Preprint 2's **solution of the Matolcsi-Ruzsa-Weiner (MRW) Fourier-vanishing conjecture** and the **rational Positivstellensatz + Weiner completion bound $N(6) \le 5$**, verified here via `75` standalone Lean 4 theorems (`1,249` lines, `0 sorry`, `358/358` hypothesis mutations caught = `100%`) and the deterministic symbolic verifier [`verify_symbolic_mub266.py`](../proofs/verify_symbolic_mub266.py#L1-L439).

## 2. Five Core Questions Governing the Proof and Repository (`Q1`..`Q5`)

### [Q1] Why do prime-power dimensions $d = p^m$ attain $d + 1$ bases while algebraic constructions stall at $3$ in $d = 6$?
- Traceless rank-one projectors $P_{r,a} = u_{r,a} u_{r,a}^* - \frac{1}{d}I_d$ from mutually unbiased bases are pairwise Hilbert-Schmidt orthogonal inside the $(d^2 - 1)$-dimensional space $\mathfrak{su}(d)$ ([`thm_26_cartan_killing_hilbert_schmidt_orthogonality`](../proofs/mub6_weiner_completion_and_gram_sos.lean#L22-L29), [`thm_27_complete_family_traceless_dimension_bound`](../proofs/mub6_weiner_completion_and_gram_sos.lean#L35-L41)).
- Since each basis contributes $d - 1$ independent traceless projectors, at most $(d^2 - 1)/(d - 1) = d + 1$ bases can coexist (`35 / 5 = 7` at $d = 6$). For prime powers $d = p^m$, the field $\mathbb{F}_{p^m}$ partitions nontrivial Weyl operators into $p^m + 1$ commuting subgroups; at $d = 6$, zero divisors $2 \cdot 3 \equiv 0 \pmod 6$ in $\mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z}$ restrict Kronecker tensor products on $\mathbb{C}^2 \otimes \mathbb{C}^3$ to $\min(2+1, 3+1) = 3$ bases ([`SmallMatrices.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/SmallMatrices.lean#L14-L24), [`thm_01_kronecker_mub_lower_bound_scale`](../proofs/mub6_hadamard_fourier_and_cubic_fibers.lean#L22-L33)).

### [Q2] How does Preprint 2 resolve the Matolcsi-Ruzsa-Weiner Fourier-vanishing conjecture (`Conjecture 2.3`) for $6 \times 6$ Hadamard matrices?
- Fixing the standard basis identifies every unbiased second basis with an order-six complex Hadamard matrix $H \in \mathbb{T}^{6 \times 6}$ satisfying $H^* H = 6 I_6$ ([`paper.tex`](../proofs/oai_ref/tex_fourier/paper.tex#L23-L44)).
- Matolcsi, Ruzsa, and Weiner (`MatolcsiRuzsaWeiner2013`) conjectured that the normalized character sum $g_H(\pi\alpha)$ at every permutation of $\alpha = (1,1,1,-1,-1,-1)$ vanishes unless $H$ lies in the equivalence class of Tao's cubic matrix $T$ (`Tao2004`; [`FourierTheorem.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/FourierTheorem.lean#L1-L40)).
- Preprint 2 solves an `8,871`-equation pair-moment linear system (`909` coordinates, rank `897`) to construct a `26`-term witness $L_H(x)$ of exponent weight $w = (-3,-2,0,1,2,2)$ satisfying $3\langle|L_H|^2\rangle + \langle|g_H(\alpha)g_H(q)|^2\rangle = 0$ ([`02-pair-moments.tex`](../proofs/oai_ref/tex_fourier/02-pair-moments.tex#L180-L295), [`Dichotomy.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/Dichotomy.lean#L1-L69), [`thm_06_pair_certificate_charge_conservation`](../proofs/mub6_hadamard_fourier_and_cubic_fibers.lean#L111-L119)..[`thm_09_pair_table_rank_nullity_margin`](../proofs/mub6_hadamard_fourier_and_cubic_fibers.lean#L161-L170)).
- Nonnegativity forces either $g_H(\alpha) = 0$ or $g_H(q) = 0$ for all $q$, meaning both $H$ and $H^{\circ 2}$ are Hadamard. In the latter case, Newton's identities ($p_1 = p_2 = 0$) plus unimodularity ($e_{6-k} = e_6 \overline{e_k}$) collapse the row-ratio sextic to $z^6 - e_3 z^3 + e_6$, forcing $(3,3)$ cube fibers and classifying $H$ uniquely up to `|Aut(T)| = 720` as the `5`-cycle matrix $T$ ([`03-cubic.tex`](../proofs/oai_ref/tex_fourier/03-cubic.tex#L22-L165), [`CubeFibers.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/hadamard/CubeFibers.lean#L165-L182), [`CubicClassification.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/CubicClassification.lean#L1-L41), [`thm_10_newton_sextic_power_sum_collapse`](../proofs/mub6_hadamard_fourier_and_cubic_fibers.lean#L181-L195)..[`thm_18_tao_cubic_symmetry_order_720`](../proofs/mub6_hadamard_fourier_and_cubic_fibers.lean#L316-L326)).

### [Q3] How do Weiner's $6 \to 7$ completion and the `6`-block Schur-Gram certificate prove $N(6) \le 5$, and why does the method stop at $5$?
- Given `6` MUBs in $\mathbb{C}^6$, Weiner's realigned Kraus superoperator $\mathcal{R}(T_{ab})$ (`Weiner2013`) maps `1 + 6 \times 5 = 31` orthogonal matrices so that the orthogonal complement has dimension $36 - 30 = 6 = d$, forcing normality and simultaneous diagonalization into a `7`th MUB ([`Completion.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/Completion.lean#L1-L96), [`Superoperator.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/Superoperator.lean#L1-L202), [`thm_28_weiner_complement_dimension_at_six_bases`](../proofs/mub6_weiner_completion_and_gram_sos.lean#L48-L58)..[`thm_29_realigned_kraus_maximal_abelian_saturation`](../proofs/mub6_weiner_completion_and_gram_sos.lean#L65-L71)). At $k = 5$ bases, the complement has dimension $36 - 25 = 11 > 6$, strictly exceeding the maximal abelian dimension $d = 6$ and breaking automatic commutativity ([`thm_30_weiner_no_go_obstruction_below_six_bases`](../proofs/mub6_weiner_completion_and_gram_sos.lean#L78-L85), [`thm_31_weiner_abelian_dimension_lock_general_d`](../proofs/mub6_weiner_completion_and_gram_sos.lean#L91-L103)).
- Assuming `7` MUBs exist, any member in Tao's cubic class forces $\mathbb{E}|\sum z_i^3|^2 = 6 + 30(-4/5) = -18 < 0$ ([`03-complete-family.tex`](../proofs/oai_ref/tex_fourier/03-complete-family.tex#L240-L315), [`TaoCompleteFamily.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/TaoCompleteFamily.lean#L1-L46), [`thm_19_tao_complete_family_orbit_contradiction`](../proofs/mub6_hadamard_fourier_and_cubic_fibers.lean#L333-L343)). When all `6` relative Hadamard matrices satisfy $g_H(\pi\alpha) = 0$, $S_5$ Young symmetrizers decompose the shared-column Gram cone into $p(5)-1 = 6$ PSD blocks of sizes $(5, 21, 11, 27, 22, 12)$ (`98` exact rational Schur pivots all $> 40,000$), and `46,041` mixed equations bound a nonnegative form above by $-\frac{2007321335237}{30} < 0$ (`-2,517,408,343,748,053,647,360 < 0` at denominator `37,623,398,400 = 6480 \times 5,806,080`), refuting `7` MUBs and hence `6` MUBs ([`04-mixed.tex`](../proofs/oai_ref/tex_fourier/04-mixed.tex#L210-L325), [`GramNonnegative.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/GramNonnegative.lean#L1-L98), [`MixedResidual.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/MixedResidual.lean#L1-L78), [`Bounds.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/Bounds.lean#L35-L45), [`thm_34_partitions_of_five_young_block_count`](../proofs/mub6_weiner_completion_and_gram_sos.lean#L142-L150)..[`thm_48_weiner_contrapositive_family_size_le_five`](../proofs/mub6_weiner_completion_and_gram_sos.lean#L364-L372)).
