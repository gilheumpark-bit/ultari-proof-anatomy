## 4. Quantitative Audit of the Three Lean 4 Formalization Adaptations (`Why 5`)

Our symbolic verifier [`verify_symbolic_pi017.py`](../proofs/verify_symbolic_pi017.py#L1-L216) and the 30 Lean 4 theorems quantify the exact mathematical trade-offs between the LaTeX manuscript and the Lean 4 formalization:

1. **Inscribed Discrete Rectangle ($k^k$) vs Simplex Cube-Covering ($2 k!$) in `Lemma 2.2` (`interpolation.tex:120, 192` $\leftrightarrow$ `RectangularVolume.lean`, `WeightSeparationConstant.lean`)**:
   - The paper covers the continuous simplex $\sum_{b \in B} \kappa_b \alpha_b < T$ by unit cubes to obtain $\ge \frac{T^k}{2 k! \prod \kappa_b}$ and sets $C_*^{\text{paper}} = \max_{1 \le k \le m} 2 k! (L_0/\sigma)^k$.
   - Lean inscribes the discrete box $\alpha_b < \lceil T/(k\kappa_b)\rceil$ of cardinality $\ge \frac{T^k}{k^k \prod \kappa_b}$ and uses the unified separation constant:
     $$C_*^{\text{Lean}} = 2(m+2)^{m+2}\left(1 + \frac{m+2}{\sigma}\right)^{m+2}$$
     ([`thm_11_weighted_simplex_vs_inscribed_rectangle_quotient_length`](../proofs/pi_transverse_multiplicity_and_seshadri_blowup.lean#L21-L34), [`thm_12_paper_vs_lean_weight_separation_constant_c_star`](../proofs/pi_transverse_multiplicity_and_seshadri_blowup.lean#L41-L51)). At $m=6, \sigma=1$, $C_*^{\text{paper}} = 377,487,360$ whereas $C_*^{\text{Lean}} = 1,444,408,272,617,472$ (`3,826,375x` larger). Because the lacunary weights $w_1 \gg w_2 \gg \dots \gg w_m$ are chosen inductively after $m$ and $\sigma$ are fixed, this finite constant inflation does not affect the exponent $\mu(\pi)=2$.
2. **Euclidean Quotient-Remainder Simplex Sandwich in `Section 3` (`determinant.tex:17-22` $\leftrightarrow$ `SimplexCounting.lean:40-65`)**:
   - In contrast, replacing $m!$ by $m^m$ in the global matrix dimension $M = |\mathcal{A}| \ge \frac{(v_0-m)^m}{m! \prod w_i}$ would lose a factor of $e$ in $D_{\mathcal{A}}(H) / M^{1+1/m}$ and destroy $\mu(\pi)=2$.
