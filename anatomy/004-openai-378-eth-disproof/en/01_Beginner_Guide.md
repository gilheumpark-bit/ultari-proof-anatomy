# Tier 1: Beginner Guide: Plain-Language Guide to the OpenAI #378 ETH Disproof Audit

OpenAI #378 (The Exponential Time Hypothesis is False) contains 3,977 Lean 4 files (710,320 lines at upstream commit 4eaee87) and 4 LaTeX manuscripts. This guide explains how its subexponential 3-SAT algorithm works, how 1,408 unimported prior-generation modules evolved during development, and what changes after resolving formal specification boundary gaps across 5 formal specification boundary choices.



## 1. How the Subexponential 3-SAT Algorithm Works

Counting solutions to an $n$-variable 3-SAT formula (#3-SAT) normally takes exponential time $2^{\Omega(n)}$. OpenAI #378 reduces this asymptotic complexity to $2^{O(n/\sqrt{\log n})}$ using three algebraic mechanisms:

1. Commuting Swap Window ($M_0 = V_0 f_{\mathcal{I}}(W_0)$, [Theorem 1](../proofs/eth_subexp_interpolation_and_hashes.lean#L40-L75) to [Theorem 4](../proofs/eth_subexp_interpolation_and_hashes.lean#L116-L131)):
   - The weight operator $W_0 = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{s=1}^Q (I_D - \Pi_s)$ has binomial eigenvalue multiplicities $\operatorname{Bin}(Q, \frac{r-1}{2r})$.
   - Multiplying the swap matrix $V_0$ by a Newton polynomial $f_{\mathcal{I}}(W_0)$ keeps both the correction rank ($\le \varepsilon D$) and polynomial degree ($\le R-1$) small.
2. Exact Cancellation of Dimension $D = r^{2Q}$ ([Theorem 5](../proofs/eth_subexp_interpolation_and_hashes.lean#L132-L150) to [Theorem 7](../proofs/eth_subexp_interpolation_and_hashes.lean#L170-L191)):
   - Grouping $M$ clause-variable pairs into buckets of size $D = r^{2Q}$ produces $h \le 2M/D$ buckets.
   - Multiplying $h \le 2M/D$ by the per-bucket rank $\le \varepsilon D$ cancels $D$ identically ($h \cdot c_{\mathcal{B}} \le 2\varepsilon M$), removing the maximum variable degree $\Delta$ from the exponent.
3. Tanner Expander Hashing and $3n$ Rounds ([Theorem 10](../proofs/eth_subexp_interpolation_and_hashes.lean#L285-L318) to [Theorem 12](../proofs/eth_subexp_interpolation_and_hashes.lean#L364-L392)):
   - Each hashing round shrinks false witnesses by $\frac{3}{4}$.
   - Running $3n$ rounds gives $\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{3n} 2^n = \left(\frac{27}{32}\right)^n < 1$, eliminating all false witnesses.



## 2. Four Generations of Prior-Generation Modules and Five Formal Specification Boundary Choices

### 2.1 1,408 Unimported Prior-Generation Modules Across Four Generations
Tracing imports from [SqrtLogSpace/Final.lean](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLogSpace/Final.lean#L1-L201) shows that 2,569 files are active in the final chain. The remaining 1,408 files (35.4%, 226,053 lines) are unimported prior-generation modules:
- Generation 1 (Fixed, 73 unimported files): Proved $2^{0.9n}$ ([ExponentArithmetic.lean:21-86](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/ExponentArithmetic.lean#L21-L86)) before reaching a constant-capacity ceiling and being superseded.
- Generation 2 (Subexp, 935 unimported files, 174,938 lines): Bundled all $\Delta$ variable slots together, causing a doubly exponential blow-up that stalled at $2^{O(n/\log\log n)}$ ([SubexpParameters.lean:51-115](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SubexpParameters.lean#L51-L115)).
- Generations 3 and 4 (SqrtLog and SqrtLogSpace): Switched to incidence-pair buckets ($D = r^{2Q}$) and characteristic-3 Galois rings ([RingModel.lean:19-68](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLog/RingModel.lean#L19-L68)).

### 2.2 Five Formal Specification Boundary Choices Audited
1. Self-Disclosed Fixed Finite Transition Table (Unimported Gen 1 [FixedParameters.lean:568-808](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/FixedParameters.lean#L568-L808) vs. Active Gen 4 [FiniteFallback.lean:258-299](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLogSpace/FiniteFallback.lean#L258-L299)): As explicitly disclosed in [FiniteFallback.lean:276-277](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLogSpace/FiniteFallback.lean#L276-L277) (Class A self-disclosure), finite precomputation below the cutoff forms part of a fixed transition table rather than an input-dependent runtime loop (languageScanner in Gen 1; FiniteFallback.Scanner in active Gen 4), satisfying $O(L)$ bounds because Turing machine state counts are $O(1)$ constants.
2. Mathlib 4 Filter.atTop Cutoff at $2^{65,535}$ ([Asymptotics.lean:18-65](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLogSpace/Asymptotics.lean#L18-L65), [NativeLargeEntry.lean:48-61](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLogSpace/NativeLargeEntry.lean#L48-L61), [Final.lean:44-82](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLogSpace/Final.lean#L44-L82)): Because sparsifySchedule n is 0 until $n$ reaches $2^{65,536}$ (and NativeLargeEntry.lean requires $2 \le \text{sparsifySchedule } n$, holding only from $2^{209,952}$ upward), NativeLargeEntry.lean uses standard Mathlib 4 Filter.atTop reasoning (Gate 5) to extract an existence cutoff above $2^{65,535}$ and route smaller instances to FiniteFallback.
3. Class B Specification Gap Between Final.lean and SubexponentialSAT.lean ([ComparatorChallenges/SubexponentialSAT.lean:55, 136-140, 216-217](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/ComparatorChallenges/SubexponentialSAT.lean#L55), [Final.lean:104-148](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLogSpace/Final.lean#L104-L148)): While [docs/378.md:9-11](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/docs/378.md#L9-L11) discloses canonical input encoding and internal [Final.lean:104-148](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLogSpace/Final.lean#L104-L148) (exact_count_eth) bounds space by $n + F.\mathrm{card} + 1$, the public comparator theorem exact_count_and_sat (SubexponentialSAT.lean:194-218) writes visitedSpace $\le S(L+1)^e$ in terms of $L = \text{formulaBitLength } F$ alone. Because the empty formula $F = \emptyset$ on $n$ variables has $2^n$ solutions requiring an $(n+1)$-bit output ($\text{visitedSpace} \ge n$), an $L$-only bound is impossible under binary encoding ($L = \Theta(\log n)$) and forces unary encodeNat ($L \ge n$, [NativeLargeEntry.lean:63](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLogSpace/NativeLargeEntry.lean#L63)). Together with the non-uniform finite transition table below $n < 2^{33,554,431}$, this forms the Class B specification gap (TIER F on comparator spec conformance).
4. Omega Slack-Constant Padding ([Theorem 16](../proofs/eth_subexp_interpolation_and_hashes.lean#L450-L463)): Standard Mathlib 4 omega arithmetic padding (+ 28700 and + 5022741, Gate 5) closes linear bounds in one step without altering the asymptotic complexity class.
5. Self-Disclosed Char-3 (Lean, Engine I) vs. Char-0 (LaTeX, Engine II) Scope Split ([lean/docs/378.md:9-11](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/docs/378.md#L9-L11), [Theorem 30](../proofs/eth_tensor_contraction_and_circuits.lean#L369-L393), [Theorem 31](../proofs/eth_tensor_contraction_and_circuits.lean#L394-L419)): As explicitly documented in [lean/docs/378.md:9-11](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/docs/378.md#L9-L11) (Class A self-disclosure), the Lean development formalizes Engine I ($2^{O_k(n(\log\log n)^2/\sqrt{\log n})}$) over $\mathrm{GR}(3^t, d)$, leaving Appendix D (Engine II, which removes $(\log\log n)^2$) and Corollary 1.4 in LaTeX.



## 3. Six-Layer Verdict After Resolving Formal Specification Boundary Gaps

1. Layer 1, Formal Space Specification ([SubexponentialSAT.lean:216](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/ComparatorChallenges/SubexponentialSAT.lean#L216) vs. [Final.lean:104-148](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLogSpace/Final.lean#L104-L148)): Under unary padding ($L \ge n$), the $L$-only visitedSpace bound holds. Under binary encoding ($L = \Theta(\log n)$, where 49,000 < 1,048,576 at $n = 2^{20}$), writing the $n$-bit output violates an $L$-only bound, flipping the Class B comparator statement from True to False and requiring the $n + F.\mathrm{card} + 1$ form of exact_count_eth (TIER F on comparator spec conformance, [Theorem 33](../proofs/eth_five_repairs_and_four_layer_verdict.lean#L32-L51), [Theorem 34](../proofs/eth_five_repairs_and_four_layer_verdict.lean#L52-L75)).

2. Layer 2, Circuit Lower Bound Corollary ([quantitative-circuits.tex:15-65](../proofs/oai_ref/tex/quantitative-circuits.tex#L15-L65)): The paper proves $\Omega(n\sqrt{\log n})$ via Engine II (Appendix D), which is not yet formalized in Lean. Using only the Lean-formalized Engine I yields the weaker bound $\Omega(n\sqrt{\log n}/(\log\log n)^2)$ unless Appendix D is formalized ([Theorem 35](../proofs/eth_five_repairs_and_four_layer_verdict.lean#L76-L93), [Theorem 36](../proofs/eth_five_repairs_and_four_layer_verdict.lean#L94-L115)).

3. Layer 3, Finite Regime ($n < 2^{33,554,431}$): The active pipeline uses the self-disclosed non-uniform [FiniteFallback](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLogSpace/FiniteFallback.lean#L242-L299) lookup table or [reject](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLogSpace/HighQueryProgram.lean#L50-L52). Replacing that Class B non-uniform table with a uniform loop encounters [LeafParametersProgram.lean:101](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLog/LeafParametersProgram.lean#L101) (4R ≤ Q) and [CostAssemblyFinal.lean:53-54](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT/SqrtLog/CostAssemblyFinal.lean#L53-L54), running as a $2^n$ exhaustive search below $2^{33,554,431}$ ([Theorem 37](../proofs/eth_five_repairs_and_four_layer_verdict.lean#L116-L139) to [Theorem 40](../proofs/eth_five_repairs_and_four_layer_verdict.lean#L181-L206)).

4. Layer 4, Asymptotic Limit ($n \to \infty$): Because the algebraic degree-reduction engine itself is sound for $n \ge N_0(k)$, the asymptotic complexity $2^{o(n)}$ and the ETH disproof are preserved after resolving all specification boundary gaps ([Theorem 41](../proofs/eth_five_repairs_and_four_layer_verdict.lean#L207-L225), [Theorem 42](../proofs/eth_five_repairs_and_four_layer_verdict.lean#L226-L260)).

5. Layer 5, Prior-Generation Hybrid Bounds: Recombining unimported Gen 2 and Gen 3 modules trims the activation threshold from $\log_2 L = 25$ to $15$ (a $1,024\times$ reduction) and compresses the exponent constant from 12,032,411,847 to 70,023 (a $171,835\times$ reduction) ([Theorem 43](../proofs/eth_five_repairs_and_four_layer_verdict.lean#L261-L293) to [Theorem 46](../proofs/eth_five_repairs_and_four_layer_verdict.lean#L344-L374)).

6. Layer 6, Galois-Ring Solver and Five-Module Patch: Beyond Appendix D ([Theorem 21](../proofs/eth_tensor_contraction_and_circuits.lean#L120-L156)), our audit formalizes $\mathrm{GR}(3^3, 2)$ Hensel lifting, tapered Hermite reconstruction, an executable Galois-ring 3-SAT solver, and a direct 5-module repair ([Theorem 47](../proofs/eth_five_repairs_and_four_layer_verdict.lean#L375-L403) to [Theorem 50](../proofs/eth_five_repairs_and_four_layer_verdict.lean#L447-L461)).
