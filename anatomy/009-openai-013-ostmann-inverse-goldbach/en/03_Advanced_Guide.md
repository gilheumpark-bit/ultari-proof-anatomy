### 6.2 Step 2 (Necessity of Definitions and Operators - Step-by-Step Chain of the Algebraic Models)
1. Why Section 3 Introduces the Common Center Lift $h/m$ and 4th-Moment Hypercontractivity:
   - To eliminate the prime-dependent phase $t_p$, Dirichlet's pigeonhole principle lifts local shifts to a common rational center $h/m$, so subtracting two congruent elements $n, m$ in the same fiber cancels $a t_p$ identically ([thm_56_common_center_lift_difference_cancellation](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L54-L60), [CommonCenter.lean](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Quadratic/CommonCenter.lean#L1)):
     $$(n - a t_p) - (m - a t_p) = n - m.$$
   - CRT uniqueness below the intersection modulus $P_{\mathrm{inter}} > |n - m|$ then pins $n = m$ ([thm_57_common_center_crt_uniqueness_below_modulus](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L66-L85)), while high-weight squarefree character terms ($\omega(s) > K$) are suppressed via Bonami-type 4th-moment hypercontractivity, modeled by the two-mode 4th-moment excess identity ([thm_59_bonami_two_mode_fourth_moment_excess](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L114-L131), [thm_51_section3_bonami_high_weight_exponent_and_divisor_cap](../proofs/ostmann_twin_engines_and_supply_contraction.lean#L427-L440)):
     $$(a + b)^4 + (a - b)^4 - 2(a^4 + b^4) = 12a^2 b^2 = 3(2ab)^2.$$
2. Why Section 5 Evaluates the Squared Kernel $K^2$ Instead of $K$:
   - On the mean-zero subspace ($\sum x_i = 0$), the rank-2 local transfer operator $K = \begin{pmatrix} w & u \\ v & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ has negative trace, causing sign alternation across primes. Evaluating the symmetric discriminant and the squared kernel $K^2$ via Parseval removes the sign alternation and doubles the leading decay slope ([thm_62_rank2_transfer_kernel_characteristic_discriminant](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L188-L201), [thm_64_square_kernel_trace_and_double_slope](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L224-L233)):
     $$(a + d)^2 - 4(a d - b^2) = a^2 - 2a d + d^2 + 4b^2 = (a - d)^2 + 4b^2 \ge 0,$$
     $$(w^2 + 2uv) + 2(w^2 - uv) = 3w^2,$$
     boosting the slope from $t_0 = 17/20$ to $2t_0 = 34/20 = 17/10 = 1.7 > c_{\mathrm{loc}} = 8/5 = 1.6$ ([thm_14_scalar_block_leading_slope_and_lemma_5_2_contraction](../proofs/ostmann_twin_engines_and_supply_contraction.lean#L296-L309)).
3. Why Section 6 Uses the Quadratic Affine Operator $U_u F(t) = F(u - u^2 t)$:
   - To separate trivial diagonal collisions ($u_1 = u_2$) from off-diagonal collisions across the binary tree and restrict off-diagonal roots to multiplicity $\le 2$, the quadratic twist $u - u^2 t$ yields the exact polynomial factorization and Vandermonde difference ([thm_65_affine_collision_polynomial_factorization](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L243-L253), [thm_66_affine_quadratic_three_way_collision_vandermonde](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L260-L271)):
     $$(u_1 - u_1^2 t) - (u_2 - u_2^2 t) = (u_1 - u_2) - (u_1^2 - u_2^2)t = (u_1 - u_2)\bigl(1 - (u_1 + u_2)t\bigr),$$
     $$\bigl(1 - (u_1 + u_2)t\bigr) - \bigl(1 - (u_2 + u_3)t\bigr) = u_3 t - u_1 t = (u_3 - u_1)t.$$
     Paired with the reciprocal twist involution ($\sigma^2 = 1$) phase cancellation $(-a\sigma)x - (-a\sigma)z = (-ad)\sigma^2 = -ad$ ([thm_68_reciprocal_twist_involution_phase_cancellation](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L291-L303)), this bounds the single-diagram second moment by $(1 + 2)^r = 3^r$ ([thm_24_single_diagram_second_moment_three_pow_r](../proofs/ostmann_tree_transfers_and_permutation_contradiction.lean#L103-L111)).
4. How Section 8 Prevents Accidental Divisibility and Degeneracy Modulo Compensation Primes:

Companion algebraic theorems [thm_69_cauchy_2x2_determinant_polynomial_identity](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L314-L326)–[thm_70_cauchy_2x2_determinant_strict_positivity](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L332-L343) exhibit the corresponding $2\times 2$ minor/determinant identity:
