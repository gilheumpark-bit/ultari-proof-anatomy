     vanishes at $2a = p$ ([`thm_08_collision_stability_reciprocal_penalty_identity`](../proofs/ostmann_twin_engines_and_supply_contraction.lean#L177-L188)), so modular cardinality bounds alone cannot produce a contradiction (the **sieve parity barrier**).
2. **Prime-by-Prime Translation Phases $t_p \pmod p$ Kill Naive Fourier Averages**:
   - At each balanced prime $p$, the forbidden residue interval is rotated by an unknown translation phase $t_p \pmod p$. Averaging exponential sums $\sum_p e(a t_p / p)$ directly across primes cancels to zero because $t_p$ varies arbitrarily with $p$.

### 6.2 Step 2 (Necessity of Definitions and Operators — Step-by-Step Chain of the 4 Core Algebraic Engines)
1. **Why Section 3 Introduces the Common Center Lift $h/m$ and Bonami's 4th Moment**:
   - To eliminate the prime-dependent phase $t_p$, Dirichlet's pigeonhole principle lifts local shifts to a common rational center $h/m$, so subtracting two congruent elements $n, m$ in the same fiber cancels $a t_p$ identically ([`thm_56_common_center_lift_difference_cancellation`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L54-L65), [`CommonCenter.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Quadratic/CommonCenter.lean#L1)):
     $$(a n - a t_p) - (a m - a t_p) = a n - a m = a(n - m).$$
   - CRT uniqueness below the intersection modulus $P_{\mathrm{inter}} > |n - m|$ then pins $n = m$ ([`thm_57_common_center_crt_uniqueness_below_modulus`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L66-L89)), while high-weight squarefree character terms ($\omega(s) > K$) are suppressed via the $3^{\omega(s)}$ weight arising from Bonami's two-mode 4th-moment identity ([`thm_59_bonami_two_mode_fourth_moment_excess`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L114-L135), [`thm_51_section3_bonami_high_weight_exponent_and_divisor_cap`](../proofs/ostmann_twin_engines_and_supply_contraction.lean#L413-L433)):
     $$(a + b)^4 + (a - b)^4 - 2(a^4 + b^4) = 12a^2 b^2 = 3(2ab)^2.$$
2. **Why Section 5 Evaluates the Squared Kernel $K^2$ Instead of $K$**:
   - On the mean-zero subspace ($\sum x_i = 0$), the rank-2 local transfer operator $K = \begin{pmatrix} w & u \\ v & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ has negative trace, causing sign alternation across primes. Evaluating the symmetric discriminant and the squared kernel $K^2$ via Parseval removes the sign alternation and doubles the leading decay slope ([`thm_62_rank2_transfer_kernel_characteristic_discriminant`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L188-L205), [`thm_64_square_kernel_trace_and_double_slope`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L224-L242)):
     $$(a + d)^2 - 4(a d - b^2) = a^2 - 2a d + d^2 + 4b^2 = (a - d)^2 + 4b^2 \ge 0,$$
     $$(w^2 + 2uv) + 2(w^2 - uv) = 3w^2,$$
     boosting the slope from $t_0 = 17/20$ to $2t_0 = 34/20 = 17/10 = 1.7 > c_{\mathrm{loc}} = 8/5 = 1.6$ ([`thm_14_scalar_block_leading_slope_and_lemma_5_2_contraction`](../proofs/ostmann_twin_engines_and_supply_contraction.lean#L282-L302)).
3. **Why Section 6 Uses the Quadratic Affine Operator $U_u F(t) = F(u - u^2 t)$**:
   - To separate trivial diagonal collisions ($u_1 = u_2$) from off-diagonal collisions across the binary tree and restrict off-diagonal roots to multiplicity $\le 2$, the quadratic twist $u - u^2 t$ yields the exact polynomial factorization and Vandermonde difference ([`thm_65_affine_collision_polynomial_factorization`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L243-L259), [`thm_66_affine_quadratic_three_way_collision_vandermonde`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L260-L276)):
     $$(u_1 - u_1^2 t) - (u_2 - u_2^2 t) = (u_1 - u_2) - (u_1^2 - u_2^2)t = (u_1 - u_2)\bigl(1 - (u_1 + u_2)t\bigr),$$
     $$\bigl(1 - (u_1 + u_2)t\bigr) - \bigl(1 - (u_2 + u_3)t\bigr) = u_3 t - u_1 t = (u_3 - u_1)t.$$
     Paired with the reciprocal twist involution ($\sigma^2 = 1$) phase cancellation $(-a\sigma)x - (-a\sigma)z = (-ad)\sigma^2 = -ad$ ([`thm_68_reciprocal_twist_involution_phase_cancellation`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L291-L313)), this bounds the single-diagram second moment by $(1 + 2)^r = 3^r$ ([`thm_24_single_diagram_second_moment_three_pow_r`](../proofs/ostmann_tree_transfers_and_permutation_contradiction.lean#L103-L116)).
4. **Why Section 8 Requires the $2\times 2$ Cauchy Determinant**:
   - To prevent linear combinations of distinct frequency leaves from vanishing modulo compensation primes, the cleared-denominator polynomial of $\det\bigl((b_i - a_j)^{-1}\bigr)$ factors into a product of differences ([`thm_69_cauchy_2x2_determinant_polynomial_identity`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L314-L331), [`AccidentalDivisibility.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Arithmetic/AccidentalDivisibility.lean#L1)):
     $$(b_1 - a_2)(b_2 - a_1) - (b_1 - a_1)(b_2 - a_2) = (b_2 - b_1)(a_1 - a_2),$$
     which is strictly positive ($\ge 1 \neq 0$) for ordered pairs $a_2 < a_1, b_1 < b_2$ ([`thm_70_cauchy_2x2_determinant_strict_positivity`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L332-L348)), enabling the Schwartz-Zippel root bound $r\alpha \le D\alpha$ ([`thm_71_schwartz_zippel_weighted_root_bound`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L349-L367)).
