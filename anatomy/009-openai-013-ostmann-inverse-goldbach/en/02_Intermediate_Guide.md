## 2. Section 2: Finite-Factor CRT, Shifted Sieve, and the `[1/3, 2/3]` Collision Stability Penalty

Suppose $A + B =_{\mathrm{cofinite}} \mathcal{P}$, so there exists a cutoff $N$ above which every element of $A + B$ is prime and every prime $p > N$ lies in $A + B$ ([`Defs.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Defs.lean#L12), [`Endpoint.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Endpoint.lean#L8)).

### 2.1 Ruling Out a Finite Summand (`Lemma 2.2`, `FiniteCRT.lean`, `FiniteFactor.lean`)
If $A$ is finite with $\max A \le M$ and contains two distinct elements $a_0 \neq a_1$, define $f : A \to A$ by swapping $a_0 \leftrightarrow a_1$ and fixing all other $a \in A$ ([`FiniteCRT.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/FiniteCRT.lean#L7)). Choose distinct primes $q_a = p_{M + a} > M \ge a$ for each $a \in A$, and use the Chinese Remainder Theorem to find an integer $r$ with $\gcd(r, \prod_{a \in A} q_a) = 1$ such that:
$$r \equiv q_a + a - f(a) \pmod{q_a} \quad (\forall a \in A).$$
By Dirichlet's theorem, there exists a prime $p > M + \max_a q_a + N$ with $p \equiv r \pmod{\prod q_a}$ ([`FiniteFactor.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/FiniteFactor.lean#L9)). Writing $p = a + b$ for some $a \in A, b \in B$, the shifted element $s = f(a) + b \in A + B$ satisfies ([`thm_06_finite_summand_crt_dirichlet_composite_witness`](../proofs/ostmann_twin_engines_and_supply_contraction.lean#L136)):
$$s = p - a + f(a) \equiv r - a + f(a) \equiv q_a \equiv 0 \pmod{q_a}, \qquad s > p - M > q_a \ge 2.$$
Hence $s = k_{\mathrm{mult}} q_a$ with $k_{\mathrm{mult}} \ge 2$ is composite, contradicting that all sufficiently large elements of $A + B$ are prime.

### 2.2 Elsholtz Counting Window and Collision Stability (`Lemma 2.1`, `Lemma 2.4`, `Lemma 2.5`)
When both $A$ and $B$ are infinite ([`FiniteSummands.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/FiniteSummands.lean#L13)), sifting by a prime product up to $Q^{1/(10j)}$ with $Q = \sqrt{X}/(\log X)^{b+1}$ ($b = 4 \implies b+1 = 5$, [`thm_07_shifted_sieve_modulus_exponent_and_elsholtz_window`](../proofs/ostmann_twin_engines_and_supply_contraction.lean#L160)) forces the counting functions $A(Y), B(Y)$ into Elsholtz's square-root window ([`AdditiveSieveProof.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Preliminaries/AdditiveSieveProof.lean#L18)):
$$\frac{\sqrt{Y}}{(\log Y)^3} \ll A(Y), B(Y) \ll \sqrt{Y}(\log Y)^2.$$
For each prime $p \le Q$, the tail residue sets $A_p, B_p \subseteq \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ satisfy $(A_p + B_p) \cap \{0\} = \emptyset$, so $|A_p| + |B_p| \le p$. Writing $\sigma_p = |A_p|/p \in (0, 1)$, clearing denominators by $p^2$ yields the exact polynomial identity ([`CollisionIdentity.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Preliminaries/CollisionIdentity.lean#L9), [`thm_08_collision_stability_reciprocal_penalty_identity`](../proofs/ostmann_twin_engines_and_supply_contraction.lean#L177)):
$$\frac{1}{\sigma_p} + \frac{1}{1 - \sigma_p} - 4 = \frac{(2\sigma_p - 1)^2}{\sigma_p(1 - \sigma_p)} \ge 0.$$
Whenever $\sigma_p \notin (1/3, 2/3)$ ($3a \le p$ or $3a \ge 2p$), clearing denominators gives ([`CollisionStability.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Preliminaries/CollisionStability.lean#L14), [`thm_09_unbalanced_density_penalty_lower_bound_one_half`](../proofs/ostmann_twin_engines_and_supply_contraction.lean#L189)):
$$2(2a - p)^2 - a(p - a) = (p - 3a)(2p - 3a) \ge 0 \implies \frac{(2\sigma_p - 1)^2}{\sigma_p(1 - \sigma_p)} \ge \frac{1}{2}.$$
Conversely, inside the balanced band $\sigma_p = s/p \in [1/3, 2/3]$ ($p \le 3s \le 2p$), the complementary factorization holds ([`BalancedKernelBlocks.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Supply/BalancedKernelBlocks.lean#L11), [`thm_61_balanced_support_variance_quadratic_lower_bound`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L153)):
$$9s(p - s) - 2p^2 = (3s - p)(2p - 3s) \ge 0 \implies \sigma_p(1 - \sigma_p) \ge \frac{2}{9}.$$

## 3. Sections 3–4: Common Center Lift, Bonami 4th Moment, and Anchor-Code Dichotomy

### 3.1 Common Center Lift and Bonami Hypercontractivity (`03-quadratic.tex`, `Quadratic/`)
Section 3 ([`03-quadratic.tex`](../proofs/oai_ref/tex/sections/03-quadratic.tex#L11), [`CommonCenter.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Quadratic/CommonCenter.lean#L20)) lifts prime-by-prime local shifts $t_p \pmod p$ to a common rational center $h/m$:
1. **Shift Cancellation and CRT Uniqueness (`CommonCenterCongruences.lean`, `CommonCenterLiftBounds.lean`)**: When two elements $n, m$ satisfy $n - a t_p \equiv m - a t_p \pmod p$, the shift $a t_p$ cancels identically: $(n - a t_p) - (m - a t_p) = n - m$ ([`CommonCenterCongruences.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Quadratic/CommonCenterCongruences.lean#L14), [`thm_56_common_center_lift_difference_cancellation`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L54)). Hence if the intersection prime product $P_{\mathrm{inter}} = \prod_{p \in I} p$ divides $n - m$ and $|n - m| < P_{\mathrm{inter}}$, CRT uniqueness forces $n = m$ ([`CommonCenterLiftBounds.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Quadratic/CommonCenterLiftBounds.lean#L12), [`thm_57_common_center_crt_uniqueness_below_modulus`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L66)).
2. **Cauchy-Schwarz Incidence Variance and Bonami 4th Moment (`thm_58`–`thm_60`)**: Combining the 3-center Cauchy-Schwarz identity $3(I_1^2 + I_2^2 + I_3^2) - (I_1 + I_2 + I_3)^2 = (I_1 - I_2)^2 + (I_2 - I_3)^2 + (I_3 - I_1)^2 \ge 0$ ([`thm_58_incidence_second_moment_cauchy_schwarz`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L90)), the Bonami two-mode 4th-moment expansion $(a+b)^4 + (a-b)^4 = 2(a^4 + b^4) + 12a^2 b^2 \ge 2(a^4 + b^4)$ ([`thm_59_bonami_two_mode_fourth_moment_excess`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L114)), and Heath-Brown's bilinear square completion $(u+v)^2 - (u-v)^2 = 4uv$ ([`thm_60_heath_brown_bilinear_square_defect`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L136)) yields the high-weight Bonami decay coefficient $-\frac{\tau}{8\mu_0}K = -625K$ ([`thm_51_section3_bonami_high_weight_exponent_and_divisor_cap`](../proofs/ostmann_twin_engines_and_supply_contraction.lean#L413)).

### 3.2 Section 4: One-Sided Square-Character Anchor Dichotomy (`04-characters.tex`, `Characters/`)

## 4. Section 5: $2\times 2$ Local Kernel Schur Complement, Double Slope $2t_0$, and `-11/125` Contraction

On the doubly-logarithmic prime band $\mathcal{B}_L = \{p : \frac{1}{20}L \le \log\log p \le \frac{9}{10}L\}$, Mertens' theorem gives harmonic width $\frac{17}{20}L = 0.85L$ ([`NonsparseSupply.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Supply/NonsparseSupply.lean#L17), [`thm_12_harmonic_band_mertens_budget_and_sparse_complement`](../proofs/ostmann_twin_engines_and_supply_contraction.lean#L240)).
1. **Characteristic Polynomial and Schur Complement of the $2\times 2$ Rank-2 Kernel (`BalancedKernelBlocks.lean`, `thm_62`–`thm_64`)**:
   A symmetric $2\times 2$ kernel block $\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ b & d \end{pmatrix}$ has characteristic polynomial $(\lambda - a)(\lambda - d) - b^2 = \lambda^2 - (a+d)\lambda + (ad - b^2)$ with non-negative discriminant $\Delta = (a+d)^2 - 4(ad - b^2) = (a-d)^2 + 4b^2 \ge 0$ ([`thm_62_rank2_transfer_kernel_characteristic_discriminant`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L188)) and Schur complement square completion $(ax + by)^2 + (ad - b^2)y^2 \ge 0$ whenever $b^2 \le ad$ ([`thm_63_schur_complement_quadratic_contraction`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L206)). Squaring the local operator $K = \begin{pmatrix} w & u \\ v & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ gives $K^2 = \begin{pmatrix} w^2 + uv & wu \\ wv & uv \end{pmatrix}$ with trace $w^2 + 2uv \le 3w^2$ ($0 \le uv \le w^2$), explaining why at $u = v = 1$ the leading decay slope doubles from $t_0 = \frac{17}{20}$ to $2t_0 = \frac{34}{20} = \frac{17}{10} = 1.7$ ([`LocalSparseKernelContraction.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Supply/LocalSparseKernelContraction.lean#L15), [`ContractionConstants.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Supply/ContractionConstants.lean#L8), [`thm_64_square_kernel_trace_and_double_slope`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L224), [`thm_14_scalar_block_leading_slope_and_lemma_5_2_contraction`](../proofs/ostmann_twin_engines_and_supply_contraction.lean#L282)).

## 5. Sections 6–9: Affine Collision Factorization, $2\times 2$ Cauchy Determinant, and `-7/20 log r` Contradiction

1. **Section 6 Affine Operator Collision Factorization and Vandermonde Root Multiplicity $\le 2$ (`06-tree.tex`, `Tree/AffinePairAlgebra.lean`, `Tree/AffinePairIdentity.lean`)**:
   For the affine operator $U_u F(t) = F(u - u^2 t)$, the collision condition $u_1 - u_1^2 t = u_2 - u_2^2 t$ factors algebraically as ([`AffinePairAlgebra.lean`](../proofs/oai_ref/lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Ostmann/Tree/AffinePairAlgebra.lean#L10), [`thm_65_affine_collision_polynomial_factorization`](../proofs/ostmann_algebraic_engines_and_cauchy_determinant.lean#L243)):
